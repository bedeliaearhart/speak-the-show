    What is real and what is not

    • AR is aligned by hand to a spike mark, unanchored to GPS. GPS anchoring needs a native app (ArcGIS WorldScaleSceneView or Niantic VPS).
    • The catalog is typed in by hand from manufacturer sheets and public rental listings.
    • Prices are public listings and campus rate sheets. Show-grade gear and HOLOGAUZE are quote only.
    • The scrim price is a generic white scrim listing standing in for holo gauze.
    • Brightness is lumens over image area against the room light you entered, before screen gain.
    • Power uses the 80 percent rule on 20 A circuits. An electrician confirms the real circuits.
    • Capacity uses the IBC standing factor. The fire marshal sets the real number.
    • Floor load on a second floor is not checked. A structural engineer signs off.
    • Cable lengths come from the layout plus 20 percent slack, rounded up to stock lengths.
    • 3D shapes are simplified stand-ins at true size. Photoreal models need the makers' 3D files.
    • Claude picks quantities and writes the walkthrough. Every number comes from the catalog.
    • The ElevenLabs voice agent passes your words to the same planner as the Plan button.
